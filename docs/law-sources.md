# Legal context and reporting channels: research notes (checked 11 Oct 2026)

Source notes behind the "Law by area" and "How to report" sections of `site/metodologia.html`. Re-check before
quoting sanctions or dates, and update both files together.

## Andalucía
- **Decreto 28/2016, amended by Decreto 31/2024** (BOJA nº 24, 2 Feb 2024; in force 22 Feb 2024). Renamed VFT → VUT.
  Art. 2.2 lets councils cap VUTs per building, area or period for overriding public-interest reasons. Limits:
  15 places for a whole dwelling, 6 for rooms, 14 m² per place, 25 m² minimum, air conditioning and heating.
  https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/boja/2024/24/50
- **Decreto-ley 1/2025** (in force 4 Mar 2025), art. 6: residential zoning alone no longer allows tourist use where
  a council cites overriding reasons. Councils may suspend new VUTs while they amend their plans.
- **RTA code in adverts.** The Junta FAQ says the code must appear in all advertising, including platforms. We did
  not find the exact article, so we cite the FAQ.
  https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/organismos/turismojusticiadesregulacionyadministracionlocal/areas/turismo/registro-turismo/establecimientos-servicios/paginas/faq-viviendas-turismo.html
- **Ley 13/2011 del Turismo de Andalucía.**
  - Unregistered ("clandestine") activity is a grave infringement (art. 71.1).
  - Fines (art. 78): leve up to €2,000; grave €2,001–18,000; muy grave €18,001–150,000.
  - https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2012-876
- **Ley del Turismo Sostenible de Andalucía.** Not enacted: the bill lapsed when Parliament was dissolved
  (Decreto del Presidente 2/2026, 23 Mar 2026). Ley 13/2011 still applies.

## Spain
- **RD 1312/2024, national single registry (NRUA).** Annulled by Supreme Court judgment 620/2026 of 19 May 2026
  (BOE-A-2026-12300) for lack of state competence. The data-sharing "ventanilla única" survives. The regional RTA
  code remains the compulsory identifier.
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-12300
- **LO 1/2025 reform of the Ley de Propiedad Horizontal** (in force 3 Apr 2025). A new VUT in a building needs the
  community's express approval by 3/5 of owners and quotas (art. 7.3, 17.12). Communities can also raise the flat's
  fees by up to 20%.

## Málaga city
1. **Instrucción 1/2024** (from 10 Jun 2024): new VUTs need an independent entrance.
   https://urbanismo.malaga.eu/viviendas-de-uso-turistico/
2. **PGOU change, DA 3ª (PL 51/2024).**
   - Effective 14 Jan 2025 (BOP 13 Jan 2025); final approval BOP 6 Aug 2025.
   - The cap is 8% of homes per barrio. 43 barrios are closed to new VUTs and 32 are open up to the 8% cap.
   - Upheld by the TSJA on 18 Feb 2026 (finality not confirmed).
3. **Citywide suspension of new VUTs.**
   - Junta de Gobierno Local, 14 Aug 2025; BOP 22 Aug 2025; effective 23 Aug 2025.
   - Lasts up to 3 years, or until the PGOU change is approved.
   - https://www.malaga.eu/visorcontenido/ANUDocumentDisplayer/178062/COMUNICADO.pdf
4. **July 2026 moratorium on "hospedaje" uses** (hotels, hostels, apartamentos turísticos) on residential land:
   effective about 25 Jul 2026, for up to 3 years.

## Sevilla city
Checked 16 Oct 2026. Verified against Gerencia de Urbanismo y Medio Ambiente (GUMA) documents unless marked "press".
1. **10% cap per barrio** (acuerdo del Pleno under Decreto 28/2016 as amended by Decreto 31/2024).
   - Pleno 17 Oct 2024 (final approval); BOP de Sevilla nº 210, 28 Oct 2024; in force 29 Oct 2024.
   - Cap: VUTs may not exceed 10% of "viviendas familiares disponibles" in each of the 108 barrios. Where reached or
     exceeded, no new authorisations.
   - Zone 1 (over 10%, 11 barrios): Santa Cruz, Arenal, Alfalfa, San Bartolomé, Feria, Encarnación-Regina,
     Santa Catalina, San Lorenzo, San Gil, San Vicente, Triana Casco Antiguo. Zone 2 (5–10%, controlled growth):
     San Julián, El Museo, San Bernardo. Zone 3 (<5%): the other 94.
   - Review one year after entry into force, then every four years. No adopted review found.
   - Instrucción del Gerente 10 Dec 2025 (CE 16 Dec 2025, Expte. 120/2025 DLIM) confirms the BOP data and says the cap
     also applies to VUTs let by the room.
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/noticias/limite-a-las-viviendas-de-uso-turistico
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/licencias/circulares-interpretativas/viviendas-de-usos-turisticos/limitacion-de-viviendas-de-uso-turistico
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/licencias/circulares-interpretativas/viviendas-de-usos-turisticos/notificacion-ce-sobre-vut-limitacion-3477-00000002.pdf
2. **Modificación Puntual 44 del TR del PGOU** (Pleno, 28 Apr 2022). VUTs are subject to the conditions of the
   terciario "hospedaje" use and its compatibility rules with residential use. A change of use must be legalised with
   GUMA (declaración responsable without works, under art. 138 LISTA; licence if works).
   - GUMA criteria (informe 5 Mar 2024, CE 13 Mar 2024): generally only ground and first floor unless the whole
     building becomes hospedaje (PGOU art. 6.2.4); lift required even on the first floor; max two units per operator;
     building must have filed its ITE; no change of use for "asimilado a fuera de ordenación". Applies to applications
     from 23 Feb 2024. Clarification of 5 Jul 2024 on room sizes (PGOU art. 6.3.3) and accessibility.
   - The Mar 2024 criteria said room-letting VUTs stay residential (no change of use); the Dec 2025 instruction
     extends the barrio cap to them.
   - **Regularisation** (instruction 10 Dec 2025, CE 16 Dec 2025): VUTs registered with the Junta whose activity began
     before 29 Oct 2024 and that meet the planning conditions may legalise the change of use. No deadline stated.
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/atencion-al-ciudadano/guia-de-tramitacion/viviendas-fines-turisticos
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/licencias/circulares-interpretativas/viviendas-de-usos-turisticos/requisitos-urbanisticos-necesarios-para-implantar-vft.pdf
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/licencias/circulares-interpretativas/viviendas-de-usos-turisticos/aclaracion-requisitos-urbanisticos-vut-julio-2024.pdf
3. **No city-wide suspension.** None found as of 16 Oct 2026. Press (July 2026): PSOE asked for a halt on new
   licences; the city government said it had exhausted its legal margin.
   https://licenciasyproyectos.com/sevilla-agota-margen-legal-viviendas-turisticas-frenar-licencias/ (press)

## Granada city
Checked 16 Oct 2026. Items 1–2 verified against the BOP / municipal edicto text; item 3 is press only.
1. **10% cap per zone** (acuerdo del Pleno under Decreto 28/2016 as amended by Decreto 31/2024, "razón imperiosa de
   interés general"; Expte. 32946/24).
   - Pleno 30 May 2025; BOP de Granada nº 141, 28 Jul 2025, anuncio 2025040994, CVE BOP-GRA-2025-141016.
   - Max 10% VUTs per zone (área estadística), based on the "Informe impacto vivienda turística" of 31 Oct 2024.
   - "Situación A" (no new VUT authorisations): Fígares (Ronda district, PTRR 24%), Albaicín (24%), Centro-Sagrario
     (23%), Realejo-San Matías (13%).
   - Review "cuando sea necesario"; no fixed review date. Sent to the Consejería de Turismo. No court ruling on it found.
   - BOP site: https://bop.dipgra.es (search by CVE). Copy of the BOP page:
     https://albayzin.info/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Edicto-BOP-limites-VUT-2025.pdf
2. **PGOU innovation + suspension of change-of-use licences** (Expte. 15569/2024).
   - Avance: Junta de Gobierno Local 26 Jul 2024. Initial approval: Junta de Gobierno Local 31 Jan 2025.
   - The innovation turns "alojamiento hotelero" into "alojamiento turístico (hospedaje)" in the PGOU (art. 6.2.7),
     with conditions such as independent access, lift and toilet ratios.
   - The same agreement suspends, city-wide, licences for change of use to "residencial singular" for homes or premises
     intended as VUTs (LISTA art. 78.2: max 3 years; ends when the innovation enters into force).
   - Public information 20 working days (edicto Feb 2025). Exact BOP date of that edicto not confirmed.
   - Status Oct 2026: still being processed (council statement, Apr 2026, press). No final approval found.
   - Technical document (granada.org, blocks scripted access; opens in a browser):
     https://www.granada.org/inet/edictos.nsf/in/OBZJKOI/$file/documentacion-tecnica-diligencia-expte-15569-24-informacion-publica-alegaciones-pgou-01-adecuacion-normativa-turismo.pdf
   - Full edicto text (copy): https://albayzin.info/2025/02/26/edicto-expediente-15569-24-informacion-publica-para-alegaciones-a-la-innovacion-del-pgou-01-para-la-adecuacion-de-su-normativa-a-la-autonomica-en-materia-de-turismo/
3. **July 2024 interpretive criterion annulled (press).** The TSJA (Sala de lo Contencioso, Granada) upheld
   AvitarGranada's appeal and annulled the July 2024 plenary agreement that applied hotel-type ("residencial singular")
   requirements to VUTs by interpreting the PGOU: the PGOU does not regulate VUTs, so that needs an innovation. Reported
   23 Apr 2026; the council announced a recurso de casación. Not final. Judgment number/date not found.
   https://www.andaluciainformacion.es/articulo/granada/tsja-tumba-criterios-interpretativos-ayuntamiento-materia-viviendas-turisticas/202604231306563248834.html (press)

## Córdoba city
Checked 16 Oct 2026. Item 1 verified against the BOP.
1. **Suspension of new VUTs in Centro and Sur districts** (Decreto-ley 1/2025 art. 6; LISTA).
   - Consejo Rector de la Gerencia Municipal de Urbanismo, 25 Mar 2025: formulation of an innovation of the PGOU's
     normas urbanísticas on VUTs, and suspension of "toda clase de aprobaciones, autorizaciones, licencias urbanísticas
     y declaraciones responsables" of VUTs in the Centro and Sur districts.
   - BOP de Córdoba nº 64, 2 Apr 2025, BOP-A-2025-948. Lasts 3 years from that publication (to Apr 2028), or until the
     innovation is finally approved and in force. Notified to the Consejería de Turismo for the RTA.
   - Barrios — Centro: Mezquita-Catedral, San Basilio, San Francisco-Ribera, San Pedro, El Salvador y La Compañía,
     La Trinidad, Santa Marina, San Miguel Capuchinos, Santiago, San Andrés-San Pablo, La Magdalena, Huerta del Rey
     Vallellano, Centro Comercial, San Lorenzo, Campo de la Merced-Molinos Alta, Ollerías, Cerro de la Golondrina,
     El Carmen. Sur: Campo de la Verdad-Miraflores, Fray Albino, Sector Sur, Barrio del Guadalquivir.
   - https://bop.dipucordoba.es/visor-pdf/02-04-2025/BOP-A-2025-948.pdf
2. **Status.** No final approval of a VUT innovation found. The Pleno gave initial approval (14 May 2026, listed by the
   council on 21 May 2026) to a general innovation "para la actualización de las normas urbanísticas"; we could not
   confirm whether it contains the VUT rules. No court ruling against the suspension found.
   https://www.cordoba.es/transparencia/transparencia-municipal/urbanismo/urbanismo-documentos-info-publica/2026
3. Press (Mar 2025) mentions a 5% threshold and extending limits to a whole district if a barrio grows past it. That
   is a study recommendation, not an adopted rule. Not used on the site.

## Cádiz city
Checked 16 Oct 2026. Verified against the BOP de Cádiz and the council's tablón de anuncios.
1. **City-wide suspension of new VUTs** (Modificación Puntual nº 9 del PGOU; DL 1/2025 art. 6; LISTA art. 78,
   RGLISTA art. 103).
   - Pleno extraordinario 13 May 2025 (unanimous): initial approval of MP nº 9 and suspension of "la tramitación de
     cualquier tipo de autorización para la actividad de Vivienda de Uso Turístico", whole city, 3 years. The report
     says no new declaraciones responsables can be filed from then on.
   - BOP de Cádiz nº 105, 5 Jun 2025, anuncio nº 82.344 (pp. 13–16). Effects from the day after publication; ends at
     the latest 3 years after publication (Jun 2028) or when MP nº 9 enters into force.
   - MP nº 9 would allow VUTs only as a compatible use in residential buildings in the "Ensanche Moderno" zone, with
     an exclusive entrance through a hall serving at least three homes, and outside saturated zones.
   - Status: tablón shows only the initial approval (22 May 2025). No final approval found.
   - https://www.bopcadiz.es/export/sites/default/.boletines_pdf/2025/06_junio/BOP105_05-06-25.pdf
   - https://institucional.cadiz.es/urbanismo-364
2. **2022 PGOU change** (Pleno 2022; in force 17 Oct 2022 per the 2025 report; the July 2024 bando says "November
   2022"). A VUT counts as hospedaje; not compatible in residential buildings in the Casco Histórico; in Ensanche
   Moderno only on ground, first and second floors. VUTs registered before then could continue.
3. **Modificación Puntual nº 10** (protection of residential land against hospedaje).
   - Initial approval Pleno 29 May 2025 (BOP 24 Jun 2025). Final approval Pleno 28 May 2026 (unanimous); tablón
     13 Oct 2026. BOP publication / entry into force not confirmed as of 16 Oct 2026.
   - Deletes art. 7.3.9.d (which allowed hospedaje "hoteles y apartamentos turísticos ... y viviendas con fines
     turísticos" on floors 0–2 in Ensanche Moderno) and blocks converting residential buildings into hotels or
     apartamentos turísticos in Ensanche Moderno, Ciudad Jardín and grade-1 catalogued buildings.
   - https://institucional.cadiz.es/urbanismo-405
4. **Bando del Alcalde, 12 Jul 2024** (tablón 16 Jul 2024): coordination with the Junta to refuse new VUT
   registrations in the Casco Histórico, a complaints inbox, and an inspection campaign (LISTA fines €3,000–29,999 for
   unauthorised use). https://institucional.cadiz.es/urbanismo-331
