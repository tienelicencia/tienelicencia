# Legal context and reporting channels: research notes (checked 11 Oct 2026)

Source notes behind the "Law by area" and "How to report" sections of `site/metodologia.html`. Re-check before
quoting sanctions or dates, and update both files together.

## Andalucía
- **Decreto 28/2016, amended by Decreto 31/2024** (BOJA nº 24, 2 Feb 2024; in force 22 Feb 2024). Renamed VFT → VUT.
  Art. 2.2 lets councils cap VUTs per building, area or period for overriding public-interest reasons. Limits:
  15 places for a whole dwelling, 6 for rooms, 14 m² per place, 25 m² minimum, air conditioning and heating.
  https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/boja/2024/24/50
- **Decreto-ley 1/2025** (in force 4 Mar 2025), art. 6: residential zoning alone no longer allows tourist use where
  a council cites overriding reasons. Councils may suspend new VUTs while they amend their plans.
- **RTA code in adverts.** The Junta FAQ says the code must appear in all advertising, including platforms. We did
  not find the exact article, so we cite the FAQ.
  https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/organismos/turismojusticiadesregulacionyadministracionlocal/areas/turismo/registro-turismo/establecimientos-servicios/paginas/faq-viviendas-turismo.html
- **Ley 13/2011 del Turismo de Andalucía.**
  - Unregistered ("clandestine") activity is a grave infringement (art. 71.1).
  - Fines (art. 78): leve up to €2,000; grave €2,001–18,000; muy grave €18,001–150,000.
  - https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2012-876
- **Ley del Turismo Sostenible de Andalucía.** Not enacted: the bill lapsed when Parliament was dissolved
  (Decreto del Presidente 2/2026, 23 Mar 2026). Ley 13/2011 still applies.

## Spain
- **RD 1312/2024, national single registry (NRUA).** Annulled by Supreme Court judgment 620/2026 of 19 May 2026
  (BOE-A-2026-12300) for lack of state competence. The data-sharing "ventanilla única" survives. The regional RTA
  code remains the compulsory identifier.
  https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2026-12300
- **LO 1/2025 reform of the Ley de Propiedad Horizontal** (in force 3 Apr 2025). A new VUT in a building needs the
  community's express approval by 3/5 of owners and quotas (art. 7.3, 17.12). Communities can also raise the flat's
  fees by up to 20%.

## Málaga city
1. **Instrucción 1/2024** (from 10 Jun 2024): new VUTs need an independent entrance.
   https://urbanismo.malaga.eu/viviendas-de-uso-turistico/
2. **PGOU change, DA 3ª (PL 51/2024).**
   - Effective 14 Jan 2025 (BOP 13 Jan 2025); final approval BOP 6 Aug 2025.
   - The cap is 8% of homes per barrio. 43 barrios are closed to new VUTs and 32 are open up to the 8% cap.
   - Upheld by the TSJA on 18 Feb 2026 (finality not confirmed).
3. **Citywide suspension of new VUTs.**
   - Junta de Gobierno Local, 14 Aug 2025; BOP 22 Aug 2025; effective 23 Aug 2025.
   - Lasts up to 3 years, or until the PGOU change is approved.
   - https://www.malaga.eu/visorcontenido/ANUDocumentDisplayer/178062/COMUNICADO.pdf
4. **July 2026 moratorium on "hospedaje" uses** (hotels, hostels, apartamentos turísticos) on residential land:
   effective about 25 Jul 2026, for up to 3 years.

## Sevilla city
Checked 16 Oct 2026. Verified against Gerencia de Urbanismo y Medio Ambiente (GUMA) documents unless marked "press".
1. **10% cap per barrio** (acuerdo del Pleno under Decreto 28/2016 as amended by Decreto 31/2024).
   - Pleno 17 Oct 2024 (final approval); BOP de Sevilla nº 210, 28 Oct 2024; in force 29 Oct 2024.
   - Cap: VUTs may not exceed 10% of "viviendas familiares disponibles" in each of the 108 barrios. Where reached or
     exceeded, no new authorisations.
   - Zone 1 (over 10%, 11 barrios): Santa Cruz, Arenal, Alfalfa, San Bartolomé, Feria, Encarnación-Regina,
     Santa Catalina, San Lorenzo, San Gil, San Vicente, Triana Casco Antiguo. Zone 2 (5–10%, controlled growth):
     San Julián, El Museo, San Bernardo. Zone 3 (<5%): the other 94.
   - Review one year after entry into force, then every four years. No adopted review found.
   - Instrucción del Gerente 10 Dec 2025 (CE 16 Dec 2025, Expte. 120/2025 DLIM) confirms the BOP data and says the cap
     also applies to VUTs let by the room.
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/noticias/limite-a-las-viviendas-de-uso-turistico
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/licencias/circulares-interpretativas/viviendas-de-usos-turisticos/limitacion-de-viviendas-de-uso-turistico
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/licencias/circulares-interpretativas/viviendas-de-usos-turisticos/notificacion-ce-sobre-vut-limitacion-3477-00000002.pdf
2. **Modificación Puntual 44 del TR del PGOU** (Pleno, 28 Apr 2022). VUTs are subject to the conditions of the
   terciario "hospedaje" use and its compatibility rules with residential use. A change of use must be legalised with
   GUMA (declaración responsable without works, under art. 138 LISTA; licence if works).
   - GUMA criteria (informe 5 Mar 2024, CE 13 Mar 2024): generally only ground and first floor unless the whole
     building becomes hospedaje (PGOU art. 6.2.4); lift required even on the first floor; max two units per operator;
     building must have filed its ITE; no change of use for "asimilado a fuera de ordenación". Applies to applications
     from 23 Feb 2024. Clarification of 5 Jul 2024 on room sizes (PGOU art. 6.3.3) and accessibility.
   - The Mar 2024 criteria said room-letting VUTs stay residential (no change of use); the Dec 2025 instruction
     extends the barrio cap to them.
   - **Regularisation** (instruction 10 Dec 2025, CE 16 Dec 2025): VUTs registered with the Junta whose activity began
     before 29 Oct 2024 and that meet the planning conditions may legalise the change of use. No deadline stated.
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/atencion-al-ciudadano/guia-de-tramitacion/viviendas-fines-turisticos
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/licencias/circulares-interpretativas/viviendas-de-usos-turisticos/requisitos-urbanisticos-necesarios-para-implantar-vft.pdf
   - https://www.urbanismosevilla.org/areas/licencias/circulares-interpretativas/viviendas-de-usos-turisticos/aclaracion-requisitos-urbanisticos-vut-julio-2024.pdf
3. **No city-wide suspension.** None found as of 16 Oct 2026. Press (July 2026): PSOE asked for a halt on new
   licences; the city government said it had exhausted its legal margin.
   https://licenciasyproyectos.com/sevilla-agota-margen-legal-viviendas-turisticas-frenar-licencias/ (press)

## Barcelona city (Catalonia)
Checked 17 Oct 2026. Items 1–4 verified against the consolidated texts on the Portal Jurídic de Catalunya (DOGC), the
BOE and ajuntament.barcelona.cat unless marked "press". Catalan term: habitatge d'ús turístic (HUT); register number
NIRTC (Barcelona numbers look like `HUTB-012345`).
1. **Registration and the number in every advert.**
   - **Llei 13/2002, de 21 de juny, de turisme de Catalunya** (DOGC núm. 3669, 3 Jul 2002; consolidated text in force
     from 14 Jul 2026). Art. 73.5: "El número d'inscripció dels allotjaments turístics en el Registre de Turisme de
     Catalunya ha de constar en tota mena de publicitat, promoció o comercialització" (added by Llei 2/2014, amended
     by Llei 5/2020). Art. 50 bis.2: an HUT needs its habilitating title before it is offered or advertised; the
     service "s'inicia quan es fa la publicitat o la comercialització". Art. 50 bis.1: HUTs are let whole, never by rooms.
   - Infractions (art. 88, greus): u ter) advertising/marketing a tourist lodging without the NIRTC or with a wrong
     number; u quinquies) ignoring an order to remove such adverts; u novies) letting an HUT by rooms. Art. 88.a)
     offering/advertising without the habilitating title (if the requirements are met); art. 89.a) muy greu if they
     aren't. Fines (art. 94): lleu up to €3,000; greu €3,001–60,000; molt greu €60,001–600,000.
     https://portaljuridic.gencat.cat/eli/es-ct/l/2002/06/21/13
   - **Decret 75/2020, de 4 d'agost, de turisme de Catalunya** (DOGC núm. 8195, 6 Aug 2020; in force 26 Aug 2020;
     consolidated text from 1 Apr 2026). Art. 131-3.3 refers to "l'obligació d'exhibició del número ... en tota mena de
     publicitat" (provisional FUE number until the NIRTC is issued). Art. 221-2.6: the NIRTC must be displayed inside
     the flat. Art. 251-2.2.c–d: platforms (intermediaris) must check the NIRTC or provisional number before
     publishing an advert, refuse it if missing, and show it in all advertising. (Art. 212-2.2 is the same rule for
     establiments d'allotjament turístic, i.e. hotels etc., not HUTs.)
     https://portaljuridic.gencat.cat/eli/es-ct/d/2020/08/04/75
2. **Seasonal threshold: 31 days.**
   - Decret 75/2020 art. 221-1.1–2: an HUT is let "per a una estada de temporada"; "Es considera estada de temporada
     tota ocupació de l'habitatge per un període de temps continu igual o inferior a 31 dies." Same wording for llars
     compartides (art. 241-1.2). So a stay of **more than 31 days** is outside the HUT definition; a 31-day stay is
     inside it. The Ajuntament's HUT page uses the same "igual o inferior a 31 dies".
   - National: LAU art. 5.e (as amended by **Real Decreto-ley 29/2026, de 6 de octubre**, BOE-A-2026-20823, in force
     8 Oct 2026): a tourist cession "no podrá en ningún caso superar los treinta y un días"; new art. 9 bis: a
     "vivienda temporal" lease must be "superior a treinta y un días" (max 12 months as a rule) and needs a justified
     cause (art. 2.3). RDL 29/2026 replaces RDL 26/2026, which Congress repealed on 2 Oct 2026 (BOE-A-2026-20526);
     **convalidation of RDL 29/2026 not confirmed** as of 17 Oct 2026.
     https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1994-26003
   - Site rule: treat a listing as "possibly exempt" only with a **minimum stay of 32 nights or more**. A 31-night
     minimum is still within the Catalan tourist definition.
3. **Decret llei 3/2023, de 7 de novembre** (urban-planning regime for HUTs). DOGC núm. **9036**, 8 Nov 2023; in force
   9 Nov 2023; validated by Parlament Resolució 897/XIV, 20 Dec 2023 (DOGC núm. 9066, 22 Dec 2023, per the STC).
   - Art. 1: in listed municipalities, using a home as an HUT needs a prior planning licence, valid five years and
     renewable only if the local plan allows it. Annex: **262 municipalities**, including Barcelona (no. 24).
   - DT 2a.1: HUTs already registered must obtain that licence within five years of entry into force (i.e. by
     **9 Nov 2028**) or stop. DT 2a.2: an owner who shows the transition doesn't compensate the loss may ask the
     council once, after four years and before the end, for an extension of up to five more years.
   - DF 1a: adds art. 187.1.p and DA 27a to the Text refós de la Llei d'urbanisme (HUT compatible with housing only if
     the plan expressly allows it; max 10 HUTs per 100 inhabitants).
   - PDF still "text publicat", no end of validity shown.
     https://portaljuridic.gencat.cat/eli/es-ct/dl/2023/11/07/3
   - **STC 64/2025, de 13 de marzo** (recurso 798-2024, PP deputies): appeal dismissed in full ("desestimar el
     presente recurso"). BOE núm. 88, 11 Apr 2025, BOE-A-2025-7430. Two dissenting votes.
     https://www.boe.es/diario_boe/txt.php?id=BOE-A-2025-7430
4. **Barcelona: no HUT licences after November 2028.**
   - Ajuntament news, 21 Jun 2024: the mayor set November 2028 as the end of the current ~10,000 HUTs; the PEUAT will
     have "cap categoria que reconegui l'habitatge d'ús turístic".
     https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/turisme/ca/actualidad/noticias/barcelona-no-renovara-les-llicencies-dhabitatges-dus-turistic-1413375
   - Ajuntament page "Application of Decree Law 3/2023": plans not to renew the 10,000 licences expiring in 2028;
     PEUAT review to start in 2025. https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/turisme/en/application-decree-law-32023-tourist-use-flats-huts
   - Ajuntament, 9 Oct 2026: **10,627 active HUT licences**; IBI surcharge on HUTs (up to 50/100/150%) passed in
     committee, initial approval at an extraordinary plenary (15 Oct 2026, press listing), final approval due in
     December; first applies 31 Dec 2027.
     https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/premsa/2026/10/09/la-comissio-dhisenda-aprova-incrementar-limpost-de-bens-immobles-fins-a-un-150-als-pisos-turistics/
   - Status: the decision is a stated policy; the legal mechanism is DL 3/2023 (no planning licence unless the plan
     allows it). **No adopted PEUAT revision found** as of 17 Oct 2026. The DT 2a.2 extension route exists in law;
     how Barcelona will treat requests is not confirmed.
   - Challenges (press): Apartur has a contencioso before the TSJC (after a rejected compensation claim) asking for a
     preliminary reference to the CJEU; decision expected late 2026/early 2027. No ruling suspending the plan found.
5. **PEUAT (Pla especial urbanístic d'allotjaments turístics).**
   - PEUAT 2017: final approval Plenari 27 Jan 2017 (BOPB 6 Mar 2017, per COAC; secondary). Annulled by the TSJC in
     2019 for lacking an economic study; the TSJC did not admit the council's cassation appeal (Ajuntament,
     13 May 2021). https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/premsa/2021/05/13/la-prohibicio-i-limitacio-de-nous-allotjaments-turistics-a-barcelona-segueix-vigent/
   - **Current PEUAT** ("Pla especial urbanístic per a la regulació dels establiments d'allotjament turístic, albergs
     de joventut, habitatges d'ús turístic, llars compartides i residències col·lectives docents..."): initial approval
     Comissió de Govern 29 Jan 2021; final approval Plenari **23 Dec 2021**; **BOPB 26 Jan 2022** (Ajuntament
     transparency page). https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/transparencia/ca/ordenanca-allotjament-dotacional
   - Content (Ajuntament press release 23 Dec 2021): HUT numbers can't grow; zone 1 and now zone 2 are "decreixement";
     when one closes in zone 1, one may open only in the contained-growth zone; **llars compartides: "Es manté la
     prohibició d'implantar-ne a Barcelona"**, except in HUTs converted to a main residence, pending a municipal
     ordinance (not found adopted).
     https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/premsa/2021/12/23/vistiplau-a-lactualitzacio-de-la-regulacio-de-tots-els-allotjaments-turistics-de-barcelona/
   - Regional law: Llei 13/2002 art. 50 quater–sexies define llars compartides (host lives there; max 4 tourist places,
     art. 50 sexies added by Llei 11/2026, 9 Jul 2026).

## Seasonal-let thresholds (checked 17 Oct 2026)
Which minimum stay lets a listing with no registration number be shown as "possibly exempt" (a seasonal let, not
tourist accommodation).

1. **Andalucía: Decreto 28/2016 art. 1.2.b** (wording given by Decreto 31/2024 art. 1.Dos, BOJA nº 24, 2 Feb 2024,
   in force 22 Feb 2024). "Quedan excluidas del ámbito de aplicación de la presente norma: […] b) Las viviendas
   arrendadas por tiempo superior a dos meses computados de forma continuada a un mismo arrendatario."
   https://www.juntadeandalucia.es/boja/2024/24/50
   - It means **more than** two months, **continuously**, to the **same tenant**. Exactly two months is still a VUT.
   - Months run date to date (Código Civil art. 5.1), so two months is 59–62 nights depending on the start date
     (e.g. 1 Feb–1 Apr = 59; 1 Jul–1 Sep = 62). A **60-night** minimum can exceed two months for some dates. Only
     **63 nights or more** exceeds two months whatever the start date.
   - Art. 3.1 (same decree): tourist purpose is presumed when the home is marketed on tourist channels, including
     any channel where the stay can be booked (so Airbnb). A long minimum stay makes exemption plausible, not
     certain.
   - No later change to art. 1.2 found. The Junta's consolidated text lists only Decreto 31/2024 and Decreto-ley
     7/2024 (water). Decreto-ley 1/2025 deals with zoning, not duration. The Ley de Turismo Sostenible lapsed (see
     Andalucía above). We found no Andalusian rule adopted after 8 Oct 2026 in response to RDL 29/2026.
   - Our earlier "≥ 60 nights" came from this article. It is now sourced, but it is approximate (see above).

2. **Spain: Real Decreto-ley 29/2026, de 6 de octubre** (BOE núm. 249, 7 Oct 2026, BOE-A-2026-20823; in force 8 Oct
   2026). Art. 3 amends the LAU:
   - **Art. 5.e** (4th amendment): tourist lets fall outside the LAU only when they are covered by a specific
     regional tourism regime. "La duración máxima de este tipo de cesión, que será fijada por las administraciones
     competentes, no podrá en ningún caso superar los treinta y un días."
   - **Art. 7.2 and 9 bis.1** (5th and 7th amendments): a "vivienda temporal" lease needs a "real y acreditable"
     cause written into the contract. Its length "debiendo ser superior a treinta y un días" is 12 months at most as
     a rule. Without the cause, the lease counts as a habitual-residence lease.
   - **New DA 12ª LAU** (21st amendment): the LAU is civil law (CE 149.1.8ª) and leaves the regions' housing powers
     intact. Its rules on temporary and room lets are "sin perjuicio" of regional rules. The text does not mention
     regional tourism powers.
   - Art. 20: adverts must show the "códigos alfanuméricos de ámbito municipal o autonómico relativos a las viviendas
     de uso turístico". The regional code is still the identifier.
   - The RDL does not amend Andalusian law (no mention of Andalucía). Read together:
     - From 8 Oct 2026, national civil law treats any stay of 32 nights or more as a LAU lease, not a tourist let.
     - Decreto 28/2016 has not been amended and still brings stays of 32 nights up to two months within the VUT rules.
     - The RTA registration duty comes from regional law (Ley 13/2011; Decreto 28/2016), so on paper Andalucía still
       decides. However, art. 5.e expressly caps the regional limit at 31 days. A host offering 32–62 nights has a
       real argument that the stay is not a VUT. **Not resolved** by any court or Junta statement that we found.
   - **Convalidation status: not confirmed.**
     - The BOE consolidated text shows only the "texto inicial" of 7 Oct 2026, with no note of convalidation or
       derogation. https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2026-20823
     - Congress is dissolved (elections 29 Nov), so the Diputación Permanente must vote. According to the press, it
       was not on the 16 Oct agenda, and the deadline is about 13 Nov 2026 (Infobae, 16 Oct 2026, press only).
     - If the RDL is not convalidated, it is derogated from then on, and the old art. 5.e (no 31-day cap) returns.

3. **Recommendation for Andalucía ("possibly exempt" threshold).**
   - **While RDL 29/2026 is in force: use ≥ 32 nights.** Brief reason:
     - A 32–62-night minimum stay is plausibly not a tourist let under national law.
     - Our rule is not to call a listing "no valid number" when it plausibly doesn't need one.
   - **Show both tiers in the wording:**
     - **32–62 nights:** "possibly exempt: over 31 nights is not a tourist let under national law (RDL 29/2026,
       pending validation), but the Junta's decree still covers lets of up to two months".
     - **≥ 63 nights:** "possibly exempt: more than two months, outside the Junta's decree (art. 1.2.b)".
   - Both tiers stay "possibly", because of the Airbnb presumption in art. 3.1 and the same-tenant condition.
   - **If the RDL lapses** (check BOE/Congreso after ~13 Nov 2026), go back to the regional rule only, at **≥ 60
     nights**. 60–62 can already exceed two months, so it stays conservative; 63 or more always does. Catalonia stays
     at ≥ 32 either way (Decret 75/2020 art. 221-1.2).
   - Not confirmed: the original 2016 wording of art. 1.2. We relied on the 2024 BOJA text, which is the one in force.

   - Fines can drop 80% if the home is handed over for social/limited-price rent for five years (art. 123.5).

**Seasonal threshold recommendation (Mallorca): treat as "possibly exempt" only with a minimum stay of 31 nights or
more.** Reason: art. 50.13 says a tourist stay cannot exceed a month, i.e. 30 nights, so a 31-night minimum is
outside the Balearic definition whatever RDL 29/2026's fate. This is one night lower than Catalonia/Andalucía (≥ 32),
because the Balearic rule is stricter (30 rather than 31). If the site prefers a single Spain-wide ≥ 32 rule, the
only difference is listings with exactly a 31-night minimum. ≥ 32 is safe under both laws, but it would label those
"no valid number" although Balearic law excludes them. Keep the wording "possibly": the art. 50.14 presumption puts
the burden on the owner, and a minimum stay doesn't prove the purpose. Wording: "possibly exempt: stays over 30
nights are not tourist stays under Balearic law (Ley 8/2012 art. 50.13)".

     se facilite alojamiento por un período de tiempo continuo igual o inferior a 31 días, dos o más veces dentro del
     mismo año." Ley 13/2016 arts. 53.7 and 54.7 say the same for flats and rooms.
   - Art. 2.1: the decree does not apply to lets under the LAU. But art. 2.2 presumes, unless proven otherwise, that
     the let is **not** an LAU let, and art. 2.3 adds that when the let is marketed through a tourist offer channel
     "el alojamiento se sujetará imperativamente a lo dispuesto por este Decreto". Ley 13/2016 art. 53.5: tourist
     activity exists when it cannot be shown the contract follows the LAU.
   - So Basque law sets **no maximum length** for a tourist stay. 31 days is only the second limb of a presumption,
     and the first limb (any Airbnb-type channel) catches every listing we check.
3. **E and L registrations; letting by rooms (verified, last bullet is our reading).**
   - **E, whole dwelling:** Decreto 101/2018 art. 1.2: a VUT is offered or let "en su totalidad". Art. 12.2: needs a
     kitchen, bathroom and a sleeping room.
   - **L, rooms in the host's home:** art. 1.3 ("por habitaciones"). Art. 3.2: only an individual can hold it, and they
     must be "empadronada y tener su residencia efectiva en la vivienda" for as long as the activity lasts (breach is
     grave, art. 3.3). Ley 13/2016 art. 54.1: "Se considera vivienda particular el lugar de residencia efectiva de la
     persona titular"; art. 54.3: one holder may not offer rooms in more than one dwelling. Art. 12.3: at least one
     tourist bedroom separate from the residents' ones. Art. 7.2: one registration per dwelling, listing the rooms
     offered.
   - Both types: at most **8 places** including residents (art. 13.3).
   - **Can an E flat be let by rooms?** Not within its registration. Art. 7.2 registers each dwelling "en una de las
     figuras alojativas, bien como vivienda para uso turístico, bien como alojamiento en habitaciones", and the E figure
     is the whole dwelling. Letting rooms needs the L figure, which needs the host living there. No article names
     "E let by rooms" as an infraction; the nearest is art. 94.5 Ley 13/2016 (changing the registered conditions
     without a new declaration, grave). Site rule: an Airbnb "private room" listing showing an E number doesn't match
     the registered activity; an "entire home" listing showing an L number doesn't either (the host must live there).
4. **Penalties (verified, Ley 13/2016 arts. 93–95, 100).**
   - Leve: warning or up to €10,000. Grave: €10,001–100,000. Muy grave: €100,001–600,000 (art. 100.1). Reincidence
     +100% (art. 100.2).
   - **Advertising without the REATE number:** grave, art. 94.25: "Realizar publicidad de empresas o de
     establecimientos turísticos sin hacer constar su número de inscripción …, una vez notificado el mismo por la
     Administración turística". So it only applies after the number has been notified, i.e. to registered hosts.
   - **Advertising/letting with no registration at all:** clandestine activity, grave, art. 94.1 (with art. 27).
     Platforms mediating it: art. 94.2.
   - Decreto 101/2018 art. 26: leve infractions by hosts get a warning if there is no reincidence or aggravating factor.

     of 32 nights or more is not a tourist let under national civil law, and the Basque 31-day presumption (art. 2.4.b)
     doesn't reach it either.
   - But art. 2.4.a / 2.3 still presume tourist use for anything marketed on a tourist channel. A long minimum stay
     makes exemption plausible, not certain.
   - No Basque rule adopted in response to RDL 29/2026 found. Convalidation of RDL 29/2026 still not confirmed.
   - **Recommendation: "possibly exempt" only with a minimum stay of ≥ 32 nights** (matches `SEASONAL_NIGHTS = 32` in
     `pipeline/rta_audit/euskadi.py`). Wording: "possibly exempt: stays over 31 days are outside the Basque presumption
     (Decreto 101/2018 art. 2.4.b) and are not tourist lets under national law (RDL 29/2026, pending validation)".
     If the RDL lapses, keep ≥ 32 (the 31-day presumption is the only duration line in Basque law), but the case is
     weaker because art. 2.3 applies to any listing on a tourist channel.

2. **Definition, maximum stay and presumptions (verified).**
   - **Art. 65.1 Ley 15/2018** (wording by Decreto-ley 9/2024 art. 2.3): VUTs are whole properties let "con fines
     turísticos, por un tiempo inferior o igual a 10 días, computados de forma continuada a un mismo arrendatario", which
     have a favourable municipal compatibility report (and any municipal licence). The cadastral reference must appear in
     the declaración responsable and in the letting contracts.
   - **Art. 65.3.a:** not a VUT: "Las viviendas que sean arrendadas por un tiempo igual o superior a 11 días computados
     de forma continuada a un mismo arrendatario." So yes, **10 days** is the maximum tourist stay, and **11 days or
     more** to the same tenant is outside tourist use. This is a hard definitional line, not a presumption.
   - Decreto-ley 9/2024 DT 2ª: bookings of 11 days or more made before 7 Aug 2024 kept their term, then became subject to
     "periodo máximo de arrendamiento de 10 días".
   - **Art. 65.2 presumptions:** tourist purpose is presumed when the property is let by a VUT management company, made
     available by the owner for that purpose, or "c) Cuando se utilicen canales de comercialización turística", including
     the internet. This presumes the purpose; it does not change the 10-day line in 65.1/65.3.a.
   - **Declared periods:** Decreto 10/2021 art. 23.j (DL 9/2024): each VUT declares its operating period and may be
     marketed as tourist "únicamente en los periodos indicados". Art. 30.1.j (and Ley art. 79.3.j): the registration is
     cancelled if the flat is used, in its declared tourist period, for an LAU home or seasonal lease.
   - An earlier "45 days a year, owner's permanent home" exemption reported by the press (2018 bill) is **not** in the
     law in force.
3. **Rooms: banned (verified).** Art. 65.1: "Las viviendas de uso turístico se ceden al completo, no permitiéndose la
   cesión por habitaciones." Art. 65.3.b: "El arrendamiento turístico de habitaciones, que queda expresamente
   prohibido." Art. 93.6 (very serious): offering or advertising "alquiler turístico por habitaciones en viviendas de uso
   turístico registradas". Site rule: an Airbnb "private room" or "shared room" listing cannot be covered by any CV-VUT
   number, whatever number it shows. (Hostels and hotels are other register types, not VUT.)
4. **Five-year validity (verified).** Art. 65.4 Ley 15/2018 and Decreto 10/2021 **art. 23 bis** (DL 9/2024 art. 3.2):
   registration "tendrá una validez de cinco años"; renewal by a new declaración responsable within the month before
   expiry, with an updated compatibility report; otherwise the flat is removed (art. 30.1.h; Ley art. 79.3.h). **DL 9/2024
   DT 3ª:** flats registered after Ley 15/2018 keep their registration for five years from 8 Aug 2024, so **no
   registration expires before 8 Aug 2029**; flats registered before Ley 15/2018 renew only against Annex III. DT 4ª:
   five years to meet Annex III (which also bans key boxes on the public street). Other removals: no cadastral reference
   filed by 31 Dec 2024 (art. 79.3.g; press: 10,601 flats removed); the DG Turismo is now also removing post-2018 flats
   with no valid compatibility report (gva.es VUT page, undated). The open data has `fecha_alta` but no expiry field.
5. **Municipal compatibility report (verified).** Art. 65.1 and art. 77.5 Ley 15/2018: registration needs the council's
   document of urban-planning compatibility. Decreto 10/2021 **art. 47 bis** (DL 9/2024 art. 3.8): the "informe municipal
   de compatibilidad urbanística para uso turístico" gives address, cadastral reference, land class, permitted use and
   "el sentido del informe"; it does not replace licences; an ECUV certificate can stand in where the plan is zoned for
   VUTs. Art. 23 bis.3: on a change of owner the new owner files a new declaration with a new report. Art. 64 bis (new):
   councils may cap VUTs "por edificio, sector, ámbito, área o zona" for overriding public-interest reasons.

10. **RDL 29/2026 and the seasonal threshold.**
    - Valencian law caps a tourist stay at **10 days** (art. 65.1) and says 11 days or more is not a VUT (art. 65.3.a).
      That is below the national 31-day cap in LAU art. 5.e (RDL 29/2026, see "Seasonal-let thresholds"), so there is no
      conflict: from 8 Oct 2026, both laws agree that 32+ nights is not tourist, and Valencian law already takes 11–31
      nights out of the VUT regime.
    - The gap: under RDL 29/2026 a "vivienda temporal" lease must be over 31 days with a written cause; an 11–31-night
      stay is neither a VUT (regional law) nor a valid temporary lease (national law). That is a civil-law problem for the
      host, not a missing VUT number. We found no Generalitat statement on RDL 29/2026, and its convalidation is still not
      confirmed.
    - **Recommendation: "possibly exempt" with a minimum stay of ≥ 11 nights** (matches `SEASONAL_NIGHTS = 11` in
      `pipeline/rta_audit/valencia.py`). It holds whether or not the RDL is validated, because it comes from regional law.
      Wording, two tiers:
      - **11–31 nights:** "possibly exempt: stays of 11 days or more are not tourist lets under Valencian law (Ley 15/2018,
        art. 65.3.a)".
      - **≥ 32 nights:** add "and are not tourist lets under national law either (RDL 29/2026, pending validation)".
    - Stays "possibly", not "exempt": the art. 65.2.c presumption covers Airbnb, a host may accept shorter stays off the
      listing, and a registered VUT used for LAU leases in its declared period loses its registration (art. 79.3.j).
    - Days vs nights: we read "10 días" as 10 nights (the Generalitat's own summaries speak of stays of 10 days). If
      calendar days were counted, a 10-night stay would already be 11 days, so ≥ 11 nights is safe under either reading.

   - National rules still apply: LO 1/2025 (community approval by 3/5, see "Spain"). The CARM filing page reportedly asks for the
     community certificate (sede.carm.es procedure 1890; page not opened); Decreto 256/2019 itself does not.
   - Size: ITREM/CREM counted **608 VUTs, 2,931 places** in Murcia municipality on 31 Dec 2024
     (https://econet.carm.es/inicio/-/crem/sicrem/PM12/sec10.html). The ITREM listing on 17 Oct 2026 has **1,581**
     (7,530 places), 577 in the city proper and the rest in pedanías (Sucina 426, Baños y Mendigo 199, Corvera 99,
     mostly golf resorts). The gap isn't explained; the listing may count filed but unclassified flats that the
     statistic leaves out.
6. **RDL 29/2026 and the seasonal threshold.**
   - LAU art. 5.e (RDL 29/2026, see "Seasonal-let thresholds") caps tourist lets at 31 days; over 31 days is a
     "vivienda temporal" lease under the LAU. Decreto 256/2019 art. 1.2.b excludes lets governed by the LAU, so from
     8 Oct 2026 a stay of 32 nights or more is outside the decree on its own terms. No conflict, because Murcia sets
     no duration line.
   - But art. 2.4 presumes habitual tourist activity for anything advertised, and art. 2.1 covers anything on a
     tourist channel. A long minimum stay makes exemption plausible, not certain.
   - No Murcian rule adopted in response to RDL 29/2026 found. Convalidation still not confirmed.
   - **Recommendation: "possibly exempt" only with a minimum stay of ≥ 32 nights.** Wording: "possibly exempt: stays
     over 31 days are leases under national law (RDL 29/2026, pending validation), which the Murcia decree excludes
     (Decreto 256/2019 art. 1.2.b)". If the RDL lapses, Murcia has no duration line at all. Keep ≥ 32 as the only
     defensible figure, but the case is weaker.
   - Hosts who show only a national ESFCNT/ESHFNT number (non-tourist, seasonal) are claiming a seasonal let. The
     NRUA was annulled (STS 620/2026, see "Spain"), so treat that as a claim, not proof: "possibly exempt" only if
     the minimum stay is also ≥ 32 nights.
7. **Reporting channels.**
   - **No dedicated public form found** for reporting unregistered tourist lets in the Región de Murcia. The ITREM
     inspection runs the platform monitoring (item 1). The CARM press page links a general "Sugerencias, quejas y
     denuncias" service; URL not verified.
   - ITREM contact (from a CARM form, press-level): Avda. Juana Jugán 2, 30006 Murcia; tel. 012 / 968 362 000.
     https://www.itrem.es/itrem/contacto
   - Register lookup for the public: the ITREM "Listados de establecimientos" (Excel/Word/HTML by type and
     municipality). https://www.turismoregiondemurcia.es/es/listados/
   - Platforms: as in "Reporting channels" above.

## Las Palmas de Gran Canaria (Canarias) (checked 17 Oct 2026)
"Verified" means read in the BOE consolidated text of Ley 6/2025 (shown as "texto inicial", last update 12 Dec 2025,
marked "última actualización en proceso"), the BOE consolidated text of Ley 7/1995 (last update published 12 May 2026),

https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2025-26358

1. **The number in every advert (verified).**
   - **Ley 6/2025, de 10 de diciembre, de Ordenación Sostenible del Uso Turístico de Viviendas** (BOC núm. 246, 12 Dec
     2025; BOE núm. 308, 23 Dec 2025, BOE-A-2025-26358; in force 13 Dec 2025). **Art. 8.6:** the RGT entry "contendrá
     necesariamente un número de registro cuya inclusión será obligatoria en cualquier publicación o anuncio mediante la
     cual se oferte como alojamiento turístico el inmueble", on "plataformas digitales" or any other medium. Art. 8.1:
     once Reglamento (UE) 2024/1028 is applied, the number is to be issued automatically and immediately per unit.
   - **Decreto 113/2015, de 22 de mayo, Reglamento de las viviendas vacacionales** (BOC núm. 101, 28 May 2015), still
     in force where not contrary to Ley 6/2025 (its derogatoria única repeals only arts. 12.2 and 13 by name). **Art.
     7.3:** owners and operators "estarán obligadas a incluir, en toda la publicidad que efectúen y cualquiera que sea el
     medio empleado, el número de registro que les haya sido asignado". Art. 6: plaque at the door with the RGT number.
     https://www.gobiernodecanarias.org/boc/2015/101/001.html
   - **Platforms:** no Canarian article puts a separate duty on the platform. Ley 6/2025's preamble says the number is
     there so platforms "garanticen que la publicidad y la oferta de alojamiento se limite exclusivamente a la autorizada"
     (preamble only). The infraction below covers "la publicidad en plataformas digitales", without naming who answers.
     National duty: RDL 29/2026 art. 20 (see Seasonal-let thresholds) and RD 1312/2024.
2. **Duration: 31 days or less is presumed tourist; Airbnb is presumed tourist anyway (verified).**
   - **Ley 7/2026, de 31 de julio, para la agilización de la tramitación de licencias urbanísticas y el impulso de la
     construcción de viviendas** (BOC núm. 163, 14 Aug 2026, BOC-j-2026-90220; in force 15 Aug 2026), disposición final
     undécima, adds to Ley 6/2025:
     - **Art. 11.1:** unless proven otherwise, tourist activity (and so the tourism rules) is presumed when **either**
       "a) Que se realice publicidad o comercialización a través de un canal de oferta turística" **or** "b) Que se
       facilite alojamiento en viviendas de uso residencial por un período de tiempo continuo igual o inferior a 31 días,
       una o más veces dentro del mismo año." Art. 11.2.c: channels include internet intermediation channels.
     - **Art. 12:** lets of ≤ 31 days are presumed tourist unless the contract follows the LAU (or other law) and the
       purpose is not tourist. The landlord must ask the tenant for the reason and write it into the contract (12.2–3).
       New infractions: Ley 7/1995 art. 76.24 (grave) and 77.12 (leve) for ≤ 31-day contracts without that reason.
     https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOC-j-2026-90220
   - Decreto 113/2015 art. 2.a/c/d: a VV is marketed "en canales de oferta turística", let "temporalmente y en su
     totalidad", "de forma habitual" (two or more times a year); cesión temporal = any stay "que no implique cambio de
     residencia". No night count in the decree.
   - So, as in Euskadi, **any Airbnb listing falls under the first limb**. A minimum stay of 32+ nights only rebuts the
     second limb. Site rule: "possibly exempt" at **≥ 32 nights**, consistent with RDL 29/2026 (LAU art. 5.e, 31-day
     cap) and Ley 6/2025 art. 12; keep it "possibly", because of art. 11.1.a.
   - New "uso turístico ocasional" (Ley 6/2025 art. 4.2 para. 4 and art. 7.8, added by Ley 7/2026): planning instruments
     may allow tourist letting of ordinary homes for **at most 15 days a year** around declared events, with its own
     declaración responsable. We found no such instrument for Las Palmas.
3. **Rooms (verified, last bullet is our reading).**
   - Decreto 113/2015 art. 12.1 banned letting by rooms ("no se permitirá la cesión por habitaciones"), but it was
     **annulled** by the TSJ de Canarias (sentencia 21 Mar 2017, rec. 94/2015, together with art. 3.2, the ban in tourist
     zones, and part of art. 13.3), confirmed by the Tribunal Supremo (sentencias of Dec 2018 and 15 Jan 2019, rec.
     3760/2017). Official secondary: CNMC press release 26 Apr 2017 (says the room ban "no está justificada"); sentence
     numbers from CNMC/press, TS texts not opened.
     https://www.cnmc.es/2017-04-26-el-tribunal-superior-de-justicia-de-canarias-anula-instancias-de-la-cnmc-las
   - Art. 2.a still defines a VV as let "en su totalidad", but with the room ban gone no rule forbids it. Ley 6/2025 art.
     2.9: "El uso de hospedaje, incluido el tipo de alquiler por habitaciones, es un servicio de alojamiento turístico", so
     rooms let to tourists need the same registration. No separate "rooms" register type exists in the open data.
   - Site rule: an Airbnb "private room" showing a valid VV number counts as matching; we don't flag type mismatch here.
4. **Penalties (verified, Ley 7/1995 de Ordenación del Turismo de Canarias, BOE-A-1995-12102).**
   - **Advertising without the number: muy grave.** Art. 75.16 (added by Ley 6/2025 DF 3ª): "La publicidad en
     plataformas digitales o cualquier otro medio de difusión referida a la oferta de estancia turística en viviendas
     careciendo o sin indicar el preceptivo número de registro establecido en la normativa de aplicación". It covers
     both unregistered homes and registered homes that just don't show it.
   - Also muy grave: art. 75.3 (activity without the declaración responsable), 75.21 (added by Ley 7/2026: marketing
     tourist stays in residential homes "sin cumplir los requisitos legales para ello"; not yet in the BOE consolidated
     text).
   - Fines (art. 79.2): leve up to €1,500; grave €1,501–30,000; **muy grave €30,001–300,000**. Muy grave can add
     suspension (art. 79.3). Ley 4/2017 art. 383.3 (added by Ley 6/2025): continuing after the title has lapsed is grave,
     €15,000–150,000.
5. **New: no new VV without express planning permission; existing ones on a clock (verified).**
   - Ley 6/2025 art. 3.3: residential zoning does not allow tourist use; it is admissible "si lo permite expresamente el
     planeamiento urbanístico". Art. 7.7.c: every new declaración responsable needs a "cédula urbanística acreditativa de
     la expresa habilitación". Art. 4.1: plans must keep at least 90 % of residential floor area/homes for residential use
     (subsidiary caps in art. 5.2: 10 % of residents per núcleo and per sección electoral). Art. 5.1.a: homes must be at
     least 10 years old. Art. 10: new declarations last 5 years.
   - DT 8ª: if the council has not adapted its plan within five years (by about 13 Dec 2030), the cabildo applies the
     subsidiary rules. Until then, in practice, no new VV can be filed in Las Palmas unless its plan expressly allows it.
     We found no adapted plan or ordenanza provisional for Las Palmas (search 17 Oct 2026; press only).
   - **Existing VV** (DT 2ª): may continue "en los términos en los que venían haciéndolo" for five years (to about 13 Dec
     2030), extendable once up to ten years on proof; non-owner operators lose it when their contract with the owner ends.
     DT 1ª lets owners apply to consolidate the use. DT 10ª (Ley 7/2026): those without the activities communication must
     file it with the council by **31 Jul 2027** or stop. So a number in the register today is still valid.
   - Art. 5.1.b: a declaration of "zona de mercado residencial tensionado" automatically suspends new VV for as long as it
     lasts. **Las Palmas:** the council asked for the whole municipality on 20 Jan 2025; the Instituto Canario de la
     Vivienda opened public information in Oct 2026 (press, RTVC 14 Oct 2026). **Not declared** as of 17 Oct 2026.
