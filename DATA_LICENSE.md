# Data licence

The figures and tables we publish (`site/data/`, the CSV downloads, `netlify/functions/data/`) are released under
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)**: reuse them freely, citing **"tienelicencia.com"** and the date of the data.

They are built from these sources, whose own terms also apply:

| Source | Licence / terms |
|---|---|
| Junta de Andalucía, Registro de Turismo de Andalucía (Open RTA) | Junta de Andalucía open-data terms (reuse with attribution) |
| Inside Airbnb (Málaga and Sevilla listings) | CC BY 4.0 |
| Dirección General del Catastro (homes per building) | Attribution required: "© Dirección General del Catastro" |
| INE (tourist homes, rental price index) | INE terms (reuse with attribution) |
| Ministerio de Vivienda, SERPAVI (rents) | Attribution |
| Ayuntamiento de Málaga open data (barrios) | CC BY 4.0 |
| OpenStreetMap / OpenFreeMap (base map) | ODbL |

We never publish individual listings, hosts or holders' names: problem listings appear only as figures aggregated by area.
